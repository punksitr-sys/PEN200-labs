#### Recap
Argus Surveillance DVR on port 8080 was affected by a file inclusion vulnerability documented as EDB-ID 45296. The application disclosed the Viewer account and exposed its SSH private key, providing initial access.
The DVR configuration stored a recoverable administrator password. After resolving a limitation in the published decoder, I confirmed execution under the administrator account.

>Argus Surveillance DVR is a Windows application for managing IP cameras and digital video recorders.
