#### Recap
A PDF directory exposed an SSH private key, and the homepage supplied the corresponding account name. The scheduled backup ran with elevated privileges and trusted a path controlled by that user. The resulting symbolic link issue exposed the administrator's private key.

Key concept: privileged backup jobs should avoid following links from paths writable by unprivileged accounts.
