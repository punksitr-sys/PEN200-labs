#### Recap
An anonymous SMB share exposed the FTP executable. Static analysis found a stack buffer overflow in credential handling, and missing ASLR increased its impact. The lab demonstrated that the flaw could yield initial access.

The initial account's group could modify the Default Domain Policy. That delegation exposed domain policy execution and led to SYSTEM access.
