#### Recap
A writable SMB share exposed another user's outbound NTLM authentication. The recovered password provided WinRM access and the user flag. Backup privileges exposed protected registry data, but the credential did not work over WinRM. Restore privileges and overly broad rights on the Default Domain Policy each provided separate elevation paths.
