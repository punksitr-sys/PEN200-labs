>In Active Directory, the msDS-GroupManagedServiceAccount schema class represents a group managed service account (gMSA).
>PrincipalsAllowedToRetrieveManagedPassword exposes the authorization policy stored in msDS-GroupMSAMembership. This policy determines which principals can retrieve the managed password.

```powershell
Get-ADServiceAccount -Filter {name -eq 'svc_apache'} -Properties * | Select CN,DNSHostName,DistinguishedName,MemberOf,PrincipalsAllowedToRetrieveManagedPassword
```
