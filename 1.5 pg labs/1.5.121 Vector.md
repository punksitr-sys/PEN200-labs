#### Recap
The service on port 2290 exposed an encrypted value and a padding oracle, which disclosed the initial RDP credential. A password-protected backup reused the login password and contained the administrator credentials. Administrator RDP access yielded proof.txt.

Key Concepts
1. CBC with PKCS#7 padding needs integrity protection and uniform error handling to prevent padding oracles.
2. UnRAR can extract a password-protected RAR archive when the password is known.
