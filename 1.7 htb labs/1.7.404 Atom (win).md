#### Recap
The anonymous Software_Updates share exposed UAT instructions and accepted uploads. The initial attempt did not execute. Further review identified an Electron updater signature verification flaw. The fail-open behavior led to execution and initial access.

PortableKanban stored recoverable credentials in its configuration and Redis data. That exposure provided privileged credentials and led to administrator access.


Key Concepts:
1. Review Electron update verification for fail-open behavior.
2. Do not store PortableKanban credentials using reversible encryption with embedded key material.
