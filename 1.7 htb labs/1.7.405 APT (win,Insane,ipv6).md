#### Recap
IPv6 scanning revealed services that were not apparent from IPv4. An anonymous share exposed a password-protected backup containing Active Directory database and registry data. The backup contained many stale records, but account validation identified an active user. I relied heavily on a write-up for this lab.

Legacy NTLMv1 authentication exposed credential material that ultimately led to administrator access and the root flag.

Key Concepts:
1. The main learning objective was recognizing and assessing services exposed over IPv6.
