#### Recap
An anonymously accessible SMB share contained a security notice that pointed to CVE-2022-30190. The lab's document-handling path produced initial access as btables. BloodHound showed that ITStaff could modify the key credentials of sflowers, putting that account's remote access at risk.

The sflowers account belonged to WSUS Administrators. The lab's update-management path led to SYSTEM access and the root flag. The notes also reference CVE-2025-59287, which is distinct from having administrative control over update approvals.

Key Concepts:
1. Assess exposure to CVE-2022-30190 through untrusted documents.
2. Audit rights to modify account key credentials.
3. Review WSUS administrative roles and independently verify patch coverage for CVE-2025-59287.
