#### Recap
I used the initial credentials to review Active Directory relationships in BloodHound.
1. Judith's WriteOwner over Management exposed control of the group.
2. Management's GenericWrite over management_svc exposed its key credentials.
3. management_svc had remote management access and held GenericAll over ca_operator, exposing the CA operator account.
The CA operator account could enroll through a template affected by ESC9. Weak identity mapping exposed administrator authentication, and administrator access yielded both flags.

Key Concepts:
1. Audit ESC9 templates that omit the security extension used for strong certificate mapping.
2. Review WriteOwner over privileged groups because ownership can expose control of the group's ACL.
