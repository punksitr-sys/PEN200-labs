#### Recap
I used the initial lab credentials to review the following delegated rights in BloodHound.
1. Olivia held GenericAll over Michael.
2. Michael held ForceChangePassword over Benjamin.
Benjamin's FTP access exposed a Password Safe backup. Its vault password was recoverable, and the saved entries included Emily's credentials.
3. Emily's GenericWrite over Ethan exposed his account to targeted Kerberoasting.
4. Ethan held GetChanges and GetChangesAll, exposing domain credential material through directory replication.
The replication exposure led to domain administrator access and both flags.

Key Concepts:
1. Protect Password Safe backups and use strong vault passwords.
2. Audit delegated writes to account attributes that can create Kerberoasting exposure.
