#### Recap
Public employee names helped identify an account exposed to AS-REP roasting, which led to initial WinRM access. WinPEAS found a plaintext service account credential, and BloodHound showed that the account held directory replication rights. The credential exposure ultimately led to domain administrator access.


Key Concepts:
1. Public staff directories can expose likely account naming conventions.
2. Review saved enumeration output for signs of plaintext credential storage.
```powershell
.\winPEASx64.exe | Tee-Object winpeas_log.txt
```
