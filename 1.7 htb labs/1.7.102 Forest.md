#### Recap
An account without Kerberos preauthentication provided initial WinRM access. BloodHound revealed that Exchange Windows Permissions could modify the domain root ACL. The initial replication attempt failed because the effective ACL lacked the required rights. Once both rights were present, domain administrator access was confirmed.
