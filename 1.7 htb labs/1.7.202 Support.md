#### Recap
The anonymous support-tools share exposed a binary with embedded LDAP credentials. Directory data then disclosed the support account's password, leading to WinRM access and the user flag. The account's group held full control over the domain controller's computer object. The resulting RBCD exposure led to administrator access and the root flag.
